Work

Three things I take on.

Websites

New sites, rebuilds, and “this thing is a mess” jobs for nonprofits, small organizations, and individuals. I’ll make it, then show you how to keep it.

Make it usable

Accessibility, speed, and the unglamorous fixes that mean people can actually find and use the thing. I also help with docs, training, and sitting with you to figure out what to build.

Getting into software

I taught myself to code, switched fields without a CS degree, then taught 1,700+ other people. If you’re trying to make that jump, we can make a real plan — what to learn, what to skip, how hiring actually works.

Where I’ve been

Senior Developer at Squiz (2025–present). Before that, Technical Consultant at Squiz (2022–2025) across 50+ projects in higher ed, government, nonprofits, and companies. Before that, educator and program strategist at Operation Spark (2017–2022): curriculum, teaching, 1,700+ learners.

About Me

I'm a bit of a wildcard.

I love learning, and I love finding the shortest path to a solution that actually holds up. That path is rarely pretty. It’s usually the one that works.

I don’t have a CS degree — mine is in nutritional sciences from LSU. I taught myself to code, figured out how to become a software developer, and then spent years teaching other people how to do something similar. Asking better questions is still the job, whether I’m in a classroom or in a codebase.

These days I write software for a living, including bigger web platforms and search. On my own time I take on websites for smaller organizations, and I talk with people who are trying to get into this field the way I did.

Before all that I worked in hospitality and student support. Stay calm, listen, explain the next step. Still the move. I’m based in Austin.
